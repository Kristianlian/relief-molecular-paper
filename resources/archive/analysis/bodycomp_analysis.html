Bodycomp Analysis

Relief muscle mass analysis

This script is made with the purpose of analysing the muscle mass data, with the following question in mind: 1. Is there a difference between age and/or condition? 2. Does age influence the effect of the condition?

Primary outcomes - Muscle volume from MRI (legs) - baseline and post - Muscle thickness from US (legs) - baseline, mid, post

Secondary outcomes - Regional arm LBM L/R from DXA - baseline, mid, post - Leg LBM from DXA - baseline, mid, post

Descriptive/supplementary - Whole body LBM
