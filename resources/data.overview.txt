Overview of data generated from RNA and UL cleanup


RNA DATA

rna.dat.RDS = total rna data with participant, leg, condition (vol), allocation (intervention/control), time, weight (biopsy), purity (260/280), sample name, age.grp. Samples with no data have been filtered out, but I have not filtered based on purity yet

rna.logchange.RDS = uses rna.dat.RDS to calculate the change in total RNA from pre to week 3 (w3), and pre to post, with logtransformed data.


Ultrasound DATA

ul.dat.RDS = ultrasound data with participant, leg, time, age.grp, allocation (intervention/control), condition (vol), VLthick (vastus lateralis thickness per time/leg/participant), VIthick (vastus intermedius thickness per time/leg/participant).

vl.logchange.RDS = uses ul.dat.RDS to calculate the change in vastus lateralis thickness from pre to mid, and pre to post, with log transformed data.

vi.logchange.RDS = uses ul.dat.RDS to calculate the change in vastus intermedius thickness from pre to mid, and pre to post, with log transformed data.
