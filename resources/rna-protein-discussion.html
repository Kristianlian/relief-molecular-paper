Untitled

Total RNA

Main finding to anchor on

Total RNA increased in all four intervention groups, but not through a training-specific effect on the final level. Pooled across volume conditions, older adults rose by 10% by week 3 (95% CI [2%, 19%]) and 14% by post ([5%, 24%]), and young adults by 18% ([8%, 28%]) and 11% ([2%, 21%]).

The control group fell by 8% at week 3 (95% CI [−21%, 6%], P(<0) = 87%) and rose by 14% by post ([−4%, 36%], P(>0) = 93%). By post, the older intervention groups and the controls did not differ (−0.1%, 95% CI [−18%, 22%], P(<0) = 51%): the groups reached a similar net increase through different time courses. The only credible between-group contrast is older intervention vs control at week 3 (20%, 95% CI [2%, 42%]), and it is partly produced by the control dip. The discussion should present the training effect as an earlier rise, and avoid reading it as a higher final level.

Age: young adults showed weak evidence of a larger early increase than older adults (7%, 95% CI [−5%, 20%], P(>0) = 88%) and no evidence of a difference by post (−2%, 95% CI [−14%, 11%], P(<0) = 66%). Volume: no evidence of a moderate vs low difference in either age group or window (P in the direction of the estimate 54–68%), and no evidence of a volume-by-age effect (54–71%).

Interpretation

Total RNA is mostly rRNA, so it is a proxy for ribosomal content and translational capacity. It is not a direct measure of ribosome biogenesis, and it can’t separate increased synthesis from reduced degradation or retention. The link to hypertrophy: RNA does not follow the volume dose-response, which matches the thesis dissociation (moderate > low for hypertrophy but not strength), though in the opposite sense. Moderate volume gave larger hypertrophy, but total RNA didn’t separate the volume conditions. The earlier work relating volume to ribosome biogenesis (for example Hammarström et al., J Physiol 2020) is the natural comparison; check the reference, since I’m working from memory.

Why controls dip and then rise: biopsy effects (repeat biopsies in the same muscle, regeneration, inflammatory cell content), seasonal or dietary change, and the interval between samples. These are possible explanations, not established ones, and the design can’t separate them.

Controls start lower (418 vs 477–497 ng/mg in the intervention groups). If the control group was not randomised, baseline differences and regression toward the mean may contribute to the apparent difference in time course. Age: the data suggest at most a difference in timing, not in total magnitude. Relate this cautiously to anabolic-resistance ideas, given the weak evidence.

Methods choices that need a sentence

A mass-adjusted model instead of a fixed RNA/mg ratio. The log-log slope was 0.95 [0.89, 1.01], close to proportional, so a ratio would have given similar answers (supplement).

The purity filter (≥ 1.8), the plate normalization, and the corrected sample-mapping error, with the final dataset (344 observations from 65 completers) given for transparency.

Results are shown as ng/mg at the reference biopsy mass. Percent changes are unaffected by this.

Limitations

Only three men in the control group, and the 50/50 sex average gives them half the weight in the control estimate.

Only two post-baseline time points, so the trajectory is coarse.

Wide intervals: the absence of a credible effect is not evidence of no effect. Control-group samples may differ in plate or batch from the intervention samples. Check with a count() by plate before the discussion leans on the control dip, because the control group also falls at week 3 for c-Myc high.

Protein (UBF and c-Myc high)

Findings to report accurately

UBF: increases of about 17–30% in every group, including controls, but none credible; no credible differences between age groups, volume conditions or intervention and control. Don’t read the “likely” labels as training effects, because the control group moves as much as the intervention groups.

c-Myc high: the control group decreased (about −36%, credible), and the older intervention vs control difference (about +103%, credible) is driven by that decrease. Moderate-volume training tended to increase c-Myc by 40–47%, with weak evidence that it exceeds low volume. Say that the intervention-group changes themselves have intervals including zero.

The control group goes up for UBF and down for c-Myc. Discuss what could produce opposite directions in the same participants (small n, biopsy and timing effects, biological variation), and don’t over-interpret either.

Interpretation

UBF as a marker of rDNA transcription and c-Myc as a regulator of ribosome biogenesis, with only a single early time point (week 3). Whether the protein changes precede the RNA increase seen later is a question the design can’t answer, because there are no later protein samples.

Relation to the volume dose-response question: the c-Myc trend for moderate volume is consistent with a dose-response, but the evidence is weak, and UBF shows nothing comparable.

No age differences for either protein. Place this against the idea of age-related anabolic resistance.

Check the antibody datasheet for the expected c-Myc size of the ~60 kDa band, and be ready to explain why this band, and not the ~48 kDa one, was analysed.

Methods and robustness

Jess automated capillary immunoassay, total protein normalization, and the choice of corr.area as the primary outcome. Mention that the normalization factor was noisy and mostly capillary-specific.

Sensitivity analyses (supplement): c-Myc high was unchanged across all runs. For UBF, only the control-group result shifted (interval including zero in the primary analysis, excluding zero in B, C and C2), and conclusions for the intervention groups held. Update this after Monday’s Compass check. The design: one baseline per participant, assumed equal across legs, and the sample order randomized on the plates. Say this once as a design feature and also as a limitation, because both week-3 samples are compared with a single baseline.

Dropping c-Myc low (reported in the supplement) and the decision to remove the random time slope from the models (supplement).

Limitations

Wide intervals throughout (±35–45 points on the within-group changes), so the study can’t separate small effects from none. Small groups, especially the control group, and single samples can move group estimates noticeably (the UBF C2 run showed this). Only pre and week 3 for the proteins.

Linking the two

Put RNA and the two proteins together as one question: is there evidence of a training-specific ribosomal response, and does it depend on volume or age? At present the answer from all three is “mostly no training-specific signal, with a possible volume trend for c-Myc”. Say whether this is consistent with the hypertrophy and strength findings in the functional paper. State the shared limitation: local muscle biopsy samples from a small sample, a control group that moves in ways the intervention groups don’t clearly beat.
